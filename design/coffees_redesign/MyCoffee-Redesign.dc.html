Same UX, minimal

The UX from 1a, stripped of its rules. Structure now comes from space and one blue field; the only shapes left are the round add button, the chips and the photo. Nothing else is drawn. The coffee page and Insights follow in the same language: hero, rating and review state, price and facts, notes and rails; then the headline average, the monthly brief and the gated findings, each with its n, and the breakdowns as a rated list you switch by origin, roaster or process.

INSIGHTS
Lines removed, space kept
No row dividers, no month strips, no card borders. Rows are separated by 20 px of air and month labels are grey caps, so the list reads as one column instead of a table.
Round where you touch
Add is a 56 px circle, filter cards are chips, search is a pill, the favourite is a small circle on the photo's corner. Everything else stays flat and unframed.
Value as five pills
Five 8×4 pills, filled against a visible grey remainder, plus the verdict in words. Filled and blue is a genuinely good buy; one pill is dear for what it is.
Blue still means you
The title field carries the brand; after that blue appears only on your top roaster, the rating, value and the add button. The origin line stays uniform grey on every row — flag, country, your average — so the list never looks arbitrary.

INSIGHTS
01 — Rating leads, price follows
The right column is one ranked stack: score at 25px, price at 14, €/100 g at 11. Nothing competes for the same weight, so a scan reads score → cost → value without stopping.
02 — Great value, not just €/100 g
A five-step meter puts each bag's €/100 g against comparable bags in your library. Icon blue at the top step is the only place value shouts; “pricey” stays ink.
03 — Flags become codes
Roaster country stays a two-letter box — outlined grey, filled icon-blue when that roaster is one of your top-rated. Origin keeps its flag, which reads faster than any code and needs no legend.
04 — The date strip retires
Month headers already carry the date, so the grey footer strip becomes a short “JUL 15” at the foot of the right column. One less full-width band per row, ~14 px back each. The favourite leaves that stack and pins to the bag photo's corner, where it belongs to the coffee rather than to the numbers.
05 — Filter cards as a ledger
Equal 104 px cells, count set large, divided by rules rather than floating pills. Active card is a solid icon-blue fill — the one selected state in the whole screen.
06 — The filter states itself
Since a card replaces the whole filter, one tinted line says so: the chip, how many of 862 bags survive, and CLEAR. Tapping the lit card releases it too.
07 — Your preferences, marked in place
When a bag's roaster or origin is one you rate highest, that line turns blue and carries its average. Ethiopia at 4.5, DAK at 4.6 — the reason to look, on the coffee itself rather than in a header.
08 — Review pends here
With Review off the tab bar, the queue reaches you here: a tinted band under the title, only when something is pending. The centre slot opens the Add Coffee wizard.
Match-for-you is deliberately absent: value first, per your call. Data shown is the repo's sample set.
